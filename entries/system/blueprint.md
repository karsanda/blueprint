The Blueprint desktop app on the Developer's computer: its window, panes, tabs, review views and settings, and the background work that keeps them current.

## Outside the boundary

- Developer: the person using Blueprint.
- Claude Code: the coding agent Blueprint runs in a pane, signed in with the Developer's own account. Its conversation and tools are its own.
- Git: records the state of the Folder, compares two states, and keeps each working copy on a branch of its own.
- Operating system: shows desktop notifications and the folder picker, and hosts the Developer's shell and its programs.
